**Structure, decided 2026-09-28 (supersedes "peers"):** Ordinary Mystic is the practice
and the platform, run under the full name Tyler Martin: tarot readings for
clients, teaching for students, and tools and software (Querent) for other readers. It is
the eventual B2B brand. Tulsa Tarot Reader is OM's consumer-only sub-brand and dogfooding
ground: the first client, hyper-local, first-name-only, built to be handed to a team one
day. Cross-link, never duplicate; TTR never takes online or reader-facing offerings, OM
never takes in-person Tulsa ones. Consumer readings stay OM's front door and revenue for
now; teaching and reader-facing content grows beside them, not instead of them. The Tulsa
build's artifacts (plan, checklist, series, decisions) are future content for OM's
reader-facing side. Same reader, one hand: Geist is the body face on both sites.

**Privacy differs by brand.** On Tulsa Tarot Reader the surname never appears on an
indexable page (first name only, "Tyler"); the full name is allowed only in private email
footers and the noindex vCard. Ordinary Mystic's rules are its own; ask before adding or
removing the name there. He was mortified when Instagram suggested an OM account to a
coworker; account-suggestion settings matter to him.
