Play the game before teaching the game. Find the revenue ceiling of Tulsa Tarot Reader
and Ordinary Mystic as they stand, climb a milestone ladder to it (first $1,000 gross
month, first $1,000 owner-income month, three in a row, first $2,500 month, then the
computed ceiling held for three months), and only then open courses, community or
software. Near-term target: an extra $1,000 to $2,000 a month of owner income.

**Why:** Tyler has ADHD. The later layers (courses, community, SaaS) are dopamine-heavy
and distracting, and a teaching product with no proven practice behind it is exactly the
overclaiming the plan warns against. He said this on 2026-09-30 while the business plan
prompt (linkinbio/plans/prompts/business-plan.md) was being written.

**How to apply:** when he raises a later-layer idea, note it for the sealed plan and
steer back to the current rung. Recommendations should be about booking density,
vendor nights, recorded-reading sales, reviews and listings, not new products. The
full plan lives in the OM repo and is reopened only at a gate or a dated check-in; the
day-to-day file is plans/now.md. Related: [[tyler-and-the-two-brands]],
[[orchestrator-working-style]].
