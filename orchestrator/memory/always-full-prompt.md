When a prompt for a Sonnet or Opus session needs a change, give Tyler the whole prompt
again, revised. Never "replace item 3 with this" or "paste this at the end of the earlier
prompt."

**Why:** Tyler copies one block into the other session. A patch makes him reassemble it by
hand and risks a mismatched paste. He asked for this explicitly on 2026-09-29 after two
patches in a row.

**How to apply:** any revision, however small, is delivered as one complete fenced block
ready to paste. The same goes for the sister repos (Ordinary Mystic, AmperSend).
