**Why:** Tyler's principle is "play the game before teaching the game" and ADHD makes the
later layers the pull. The full plan stays closed; `now.md` is what he reads weekly.

**How to apply:** Reopen the full plan only at a rung of the ladder or a dated check-in
(2027-01-15 first, then every six months). When a rung is reached, update `now.md` and
nothing else. Rung measure is "ladder income" (gross less 25% reserve less direct costs);
rung 5 (exit gate) is about $1,520 a month for three consecutive months. Model headlines:
OM ceiling $926 a month (hours-bound), TTR $1,780 ordinary, $2,680 peak, $1,285 average.
Prices: Step 1 was taken 2026-09-18 (unproven); Step 2 at rung 3 plus eight weeks of held
demand; live steps only on booking pressure. Astrology is to be removed from the OM site
(checklist in chapter 29). Decisions 2026-09-30: no minor may buy a reading, but institution-booked, supervised school
events are fine as group entertainment; the AI subscription drops to the $100 tier in 2028. See [[tyler-and-the-two-brands]] and
[[game-before-meta-game]].
